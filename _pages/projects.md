  <h1>Projects that connect AI, sensing, and human behavior.</h1>
  <p>My work spans virtual sensor generation, multimodal learning, wearable computing, and inclusive human-centered systems. These projects share one goal: making behavioral AI more scalable, realistic, and useful.</p>

      <div class="project-meta"><span>Embodied AI</span><strong>AAAI 2026</strong></div>
      <h2>AgentSense</h2>
      <p class="project-card__lead">Smart-home sensor data, generated by agents that live there.</p>
      <p>LLMs create diverse personas and routines; embodied agents execute them in an extended VirtualHome simulator instrumented with ambient sensors. Pretraining on this data improves recognition, especially when real data is limited.</p>
      <div class="project-facts"><span><b>5</b> real datasets</span><span><b>Privacy</b> by design</span></div>
