Labelled wearable data is scarce. IMUGPT generates it from language: an LLM writes many different descriptions of an activity, a text-to-motion model turns each one into 3D human motion, and the motion becomes virtual IMU data for training activity recognition models. No cameras, no recording sessions.

Diversity from language

One activity, a thousand descriptions

The LLM varies who is moving, where and how. Each description becomes a different motion, and that variety is what makes the virtual data useful.

IMUGPT 2.0 · Motion filter

An LLM checks every generated motion

Text-to-motion models sometimes get it wrong: a "lying down" prompt becomes sitting. IMUGPT 2.0 captions each motion with MotionGPT and asks an LLM whether the caption matches the description. Below are real generated clips with GPT-4's verdict and the human label.

IMUGPT 2.0 · When to stop generating

Diverse text gives diverse motion, until it saturates

Text diversity and motion diversity move together (Pearson r = 0.87–0.92 across datasets). Once new descriptions stop adding diversity, generation can stop: the saturation point.
